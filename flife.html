How to Play
Each turn
1
Draw a What's Next card and follow instructions. Retired players draw a Retirement card instead.
2
If instructed, draw an F-Life card from the deck matching your education level.
3
Resolve all card actions. Turn ends. Play continues clockwise.
Key mechanics
Aging

Cross off ages on your form as you pass them.

No salary collected when you age.

Kidney Stone cards drawn at ages 40, 50, and 60.

Reach age 100 → die immediately.

Salary

Only collected when a card specifically says so.

Unemployed: salary cut in half (base job card only).

Debt

Normal — no penalty. Pay anyway and go further into debt.

Retirement

Triggered at age 65+. Draw Retirement cards instead of What's Next.

Retirement cards can kill you.

Death

Enter the Graveyard — still take turns.

Draw a Graveyard card each turn (secret, held in hand).

No more aging once dead.

Personality Points

Luck + Intellect + Street Smarts + Logic + Charisma.

Tracked on your form. Used against other players & for end-game bonuses.

Mental Disorders

Cannot be cured. Only 'Mental Disorder' cards affect them.

'All Illnesses Cured' does NOT cure a mental disorder.

End game: –1 to ALL personality point types (doesn't stack).

Specialized Medical Conditions

Keep the first one you draw. A second one has no effect — discard it.

Not illnesses. Cannot be cured or discarded.

Dialysis: draw a Kidney Stone card at ages 70, 80, and 90 before your Retirement card.

Dementia / Alzheimer's: odd age → –$1,000 · even age → +1 year.

Tacky Decorations

Work like any other purchase. Sell at card price at end of game.

Key distinctions

Addictions ≠ Illnesses ≠ STDs ≠ Mental Disorders — each has its own cures. A cure for one won't affect another.

Multiple spouses, STDs, and children are all allowed.

Players age ONLY when a card instructs it.

Scoring & Winning
When the game ends

Game ends when ALL players have died.

1. Determine Peasant & Royalty from current cash on hand first.

2. Then total everything up — most money wins.

Card Types
Backstory

Drawn once at start. Sets your unique starting attributes. May be positive or negative.

Bond

Placed face-down without looking. Only revealed when instructed. Values can be positive or negative.

F-Life

Separate deck per education level. Draw from your matching deck when instructed.

Job

Drawn at start; replaced when instructed. Must match your education level. One job at a time.

Kidney Stone

Drawn at ages 40, 50, and 60 (underlined on form), IMMEDIATELY after the current player's turn ends.

Personality

Held in hand — secret. Use any number at any time.

Relationship

Gives spouses, children, STDs, and more. Multiples of everything allowed.

Bankruptcy

Declare at any point during the game. Keep for the rest of the game.

Retirement

Drawn instead of What's Next at age 65+. Can kill you.

Graveyard

Drawn on your turn after death. Secret, held in hand. Any number usable per turn.

What's Next

Drawn at the start of each living turn. Not drawn after retirement.

Drug

Buy ($4k) or sell one per turn before other cards. End of game: sell for double value.

Mental Disorder

Cannot be cured. End game: –1 to ALL personality point types. Penalty doesn't stack.
